# LinkedIn post draft

When an AI answer disappoints, most of us ask for more.

More detail. More sections. More "go deeper."

We get volume, not depth.

Felix Rieseberg, who works on Claude, shared a prompt that fixed this for me:
"I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one."

Before rewriting the task, the model has to answer four questions:

1. What would a top professional do here that I'm not asking for?
2. What raw material would they start from, instead of the digested summary?
3. Which specific methods and tools would they use?
4. What, other than you or me, would prove the result is right?

After a week of using it every day:
→ Fewer rounds of "no, that's not what I meant"
→ Shorter, sharper prompts
→ Results I can verify with a test or a benchmark, instead of trusting the tone

It isn't free. It costs a few extra tokens, it's overkill for quick questions, and it can't give the agent access to data it doesn't have. Those limits are worth knowing.
